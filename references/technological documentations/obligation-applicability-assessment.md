# Obligation applicability assessment

## Selection method

1. Start from the actual workflow, jurisdiction, data and actors.
2. Ask the responsible policy, legal or compliance owner which obligations apply and which authoritative source establishes them.
3. Translate applicable duties into required control outcomes and evidence; keep unconfirmed applicability open rather than treating a whole catalogue as mandatory.

## Supporting guidance and sources

Treat control catalogues as candidates. Determine applicability from actual jurisdiction, workflow, data, actors and organizational policy. One person can hold policy, engineering and release roles while their responsibilities remain distinct.
