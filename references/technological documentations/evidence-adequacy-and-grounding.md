# Evidence adequacy and grounding

Use this reference when defining whether search results support a task's answer.

## Decision method

Set source suitability by authority, applicable scope, effective date and version; reuse or search cost may affect search order without changing authority.

Define supported, partial, insufficient, conflicting and further-search outcomes against the required claims. Preserve original intent and exact identifiers during follow-up rewriting.

Bind claims to visible supporting passages and citations, disclose material conflicts and use the approved abstention/referral outcome when support is inadequate. Evaluate evidence coverage, ranking and synthesis separately.
