# Online evaluation sampling

Use this reference when production quality review needs justified coverage and cost.

## Decision method

Keep trusted authorization/safety enforcement synchronous at its approved action boundary; sampled quality grading does not replace it.

Choose eligible task/risk segments, asynchronous sampling/count rules and missing/harmful-result handling from harm, traffic and required evidence.

Estimate total judging and calibration cost from per-grade resource use. Neither judging all high-risk traffic nor exactly five-percent routine sampling is universal; collect the predeclared evidence needed for the decision.
