# Output failure behavior

## Selection method

1. Identify missing-input, ambiguity and failure conditions reachable in the scoped task.
2. Determine whether completed parts are independently useful or the result must be atomic.
3. Choose a visible incomplete, clarification, failure or referral outcome that accurately describes verified work.

## Supporting guidance and sources

Use reachable ambiguity, missing-input and failure cases to decide the permitted response. Verified independent parts can be useful when the overall task is labelled incomplete; atomic tasks need a clear failure or authorized handoff. Approval and retry mechanics follow the approved policy and later design.
