# Domain semantic definition

Use this reference when queries depend on authoritative business terminology or measures.

## Decision method

Identify the owner and effective version of a term, measure or business formula before translating a request into filters or calculations.

Record formula, dimensions, valid filters and source together. Resolve domain ambiguity with the accountable owner and make consuming queries reference the definition.

Keep domain business measures distinct from agent-evaluation metrics; a semantic layer is useful only when the task's vocabulary or calculations need it.
