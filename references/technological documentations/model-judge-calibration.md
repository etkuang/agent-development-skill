# Model judge calibration

Use this reference when a model grades subjective or otherwise residual criteria.

## Decision method

Write the exact rubric and compare a capable baseline and proposed candidate against authorized expert-human judgments on canonical versioned fixtures.

Use predeclared agreement and serious-error criteria by task segment, with runner/run point, expected evidence, adjudicator, abstention and recalibration triggers.

Keep cheaper adoption pending until measured calibration passes; an unexecuted plan is not agreement evidence. Estimate per-grade calls/tokens/cost and calibration overhead for scheduling. Multiple judges are justified only by demonstrated decision benefit.
