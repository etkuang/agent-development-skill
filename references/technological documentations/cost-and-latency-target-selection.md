# Cost and latency target selection

## Selection method

1. Choose the workload and user interaction for which a target is needed.
2. Define what the cost or latency measurement includes, then choose the relevant objective, ceiling, percentile or attainment goal.
3. Check available baseline evidence and identify how a missing target will be measured; defer component budget allocation until the design exists.

## Supporting guidance and sources

Choose cost and latency signals and boundaries for the workload. A fixed P95 or gateway, router, tool and generation allocation is an example, not a universal requirement. [AWS performance-aligned success criteria](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf01-bp01.html) (Agentic AI Lens current), accessed 2026-10-10.
