# Compliance evidence review

Use this reference when confirmed obligations or required controls need recurring operational review.

## Decision method

Map confirmed duty/control to deployed test baseline, protected audit fields, measures and accountable reviewer. Choose permitted evidence samples/cadence and missing-evidence disposition.

Check completeness, attribution/integrity, access and retention/deletion execution; schedule scoped adversarial rechecks after relevant material changes.

Adjudicate missed harm and false blocks with reviewed labels and approved thresholds. Changed applicability returns to the obligation owner and design changes require renewed approval; recurring review does not invent legal duties.
