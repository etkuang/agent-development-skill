# Retrieval need assessment

## Selection method

1. Identify question classes that require external or current evidence.
2. Compare a no-retrieval baseline with feasible evidence access on representative questions.
3. Check coverage, freshness, access and accepted-source constraints before including retrieval; leave the detailed retrieval route to later design.

## Supporting guidance and sources

Compare questions needing external evidence with a no-retrieval baseline and feasible access options. Coverage and freshness determine the need; the selected direct or indexed route is a later decision. [AWS document-query options](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/retrieval-augmented-generation-options/options.html) (Prescriptive Guidance current) and [Azure RAG preparation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/ai-ml/guide/rag/rag-preparation-phase) (current), accessed 2026-10-10.
