# Audit-trail need assessment

## Selection method

1. Identify an actual obligation or approved operational need that requires an audit record.
2. Choose the event classes and actor, version, result and correlation information necessary for that purpose.
3. Apply minimization, reviewer access and retention policy; do not treat chat history or source checking as an audit trail.

## Supporting guidance and sources

Audit capture follows an actual obligation or approved governance need. Keep source checks, retained chat history and operational audit records distinct; required audit content follows the owner's minimization, access and retention policy.
