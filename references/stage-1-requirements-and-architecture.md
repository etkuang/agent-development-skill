## Requirement-to-indicator translation (1.1)

Business discovery owns the problem, current business steps, user groups, bottlenecks, baseline and first-release scope. Agree the desired improvement and canonical task exclusions with business stakeholders. Record participants' business responsibilities here; acting and approval authority belongs to 1.3 and system operation/change authority to 1.4. [AWS Generative AI lifecycle](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/generative-ai-lens/generative-ai-lifecycle.html) and [AWS agent roles and scope](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentops01-bp01.html) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.

Choose business-outcome indicators and record their baseline evidence or a baseline-collection action. Record desired business improvement and link functional acceptance from 1.2 and cost/latency targets from 1.4; this aspect does not create separate performance ceilings. Different interaction patterns inform the later latency decision rather than a universal P99 target. [AWS agent performance criteria](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf01-bp01.html) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.

Reason-act-observe, memory, tools, fault handling, observability and coordination are candidate downstream design dimensions. Record a business need or material assumption before considering one. Keep assumption owner, evidence/status, validation action and possible scope consequence in the assumption log. Release slices and business prerequisites belong here; architecture choice and task execution coupling belong to 1.5, while authority and escalation policy belong to 1.3. [Anthropic building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024-12-19), [AWS Agentic AI Lens design principles](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/design-principles.html) (current), and [Azure architecture decision records](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/architect-role/architecture-decision-record) (updated 2026-04-13), accessed 2026-09-30.

Make discovery reviewable through the canonical scope decision, user/workflow list, business-need indicator table and assumption log. Give the business table observable outcomes, baseline evidence and desired improvement; reference the functional and constraint owners instead of repeating their acceptance or cost/latency target values. Discovery research should test assumptions that could change the decision to proceed. [AWS organizational-workflow guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentsus03-bp02.html) (current), [AWS agent performance criteria](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf01-bp01.html) (current), and [UK Government Service Manual discovery guide](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-discovery-phase-works) (updated 2021-06-21), accessed 2026-09-30.

## Failure-mode framing for outputs (1.2)

- Capture a small seed of real priority tasks before prompt and architecture decisions harden. Each case states the input, acceptable outcome or end state, and critical required or prohibited constraints; Stage 4 later expands, grades, versions, and runs the full set. A fixed 50-case quota is unnecessary at this point. [Anthropic agent-evaluation guide](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) (2026-01-09) and [OpenAI evaluation best practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) (current API guide), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Outcome and quality.** Check the actual task end state and required constraints; a fluent claim of completion is not proof. Assess communication quality separately from functional correctness. [Anthropic agent-evaluation guide](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) (2026-01-09), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Clarification and approval.** Agree the user-visible gap or question for reachable missing inputs and ambiguity. For an approval-paused action, consume the policy trigger and reviewer lifecycle from 1.3 and record only the pending-state response in the failure-mode contract. Stage 2 wires the pause and enforcement. [Microsoft Copilot Studio design example](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/guidance/agent-design-canvas-example) (updated 2026-02-24) and [OpenAI Agents SDK human-in-the-loop](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/human_in_the_loop/) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Partial and stopped work.** Decide which functions permit useful verified partial output, the gap notice and overall incomplete status; identify functions requiring an atomic outcome or clear failure. Reference 1.3 for authorized handoff and 1.4 for budgets. Runtime retry/cutoff transitions belong to 2.3 and exact call replay/deadline limits to 3.1. [AWS agent reasoning-pipeline guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf02-bp01.html) and [AWS recovery guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentops07-bp01.html) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Source-dependent claims.** Agree the required citable source identifier and available page, section, record or block locator in the function output contract, including the result when support is insufficient. The retrieval architecture in 2.5 and concrete prompt/schema validation in 3.3 refine how that contract is met. [OpenAI Citation Formatting](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/citation-formatting) (current API guide) and [Claude search-result citations](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/search-results) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Shared target values.** Function-level cases use agreed cost and latency targets from 1.4; they do not create conflicting copies. [AWS agent performance criteria](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf01-bp01.html) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Stage 1 outputs.** Record prioritized function inputs, audience/output format, verified end state, communication criteria and acceptance case. The failure-mode table records user-visible missing, paused, partial, deferred or stopped states with policy references. The out-of-scope response table references excluded task IDs from 1.1 and decides decline, redirect or authorized handoff without creating a second scope list. [Microsoft agent purpose and key scenarios](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/agents/agent-evaluation/evaluation-define-purpose) (updated 2026-05-20), [OpenAI Agents SDK running agents](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/running_agents/) and [human-in-the-loop](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/human_in_the_loop/) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.

## Compliance and governance (1.4)

A layered compliance catalogue is a menu of candidate controls. Start with the actual jurisdiction, workflow, data classes, user groups, actions, and organizational policy. Ask the accountable legal, compliance, or policy owner to confirm applicability and record unresolved obligations as decisions. A policy owner defines the obligation, engineering implements the control, and the release owner verifies the evidence; the same person may fill more than one role in a small project. [Microsoft responsible AI in Azure workloads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/responsible-ai) (current) and [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) (AI RMF 1.0), accessed 2026-09-30.

For each confirmed obligation, record its source/jurisdiction/version, concrete duty, affected data/action, required control outcome, accountable owner, required test/audit evidence and applicability review trigger. Permission, deterministic validation, filtering, refusal, review or reporting are candidate control families; concrete engineering controls are bound by references to their Stage 2/3 owners. Stage 4 defines violation and false-block measures against the confirmed duty. [AWS guardrails and alignment controls](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentsec04-bp01.html) (Agentic AI Lens current) and [Microsoft responsible AI in Azure workloads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/responsible-ai) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.

The constraints summary carries canonical IDs, accountable owners, confirmed targets or pending decisions, required evidence and release-gate references. Agree the per-task cost ceiling and workload-specific latency objective in 1.4, including signal, boundary, percentile or attainment goal and window. Stage 3 allocates phase/call budgets and Stage 4 defines trace-based measurement. A fixed P95 or gateway/router/tool/generation allocation is an example rather than a universal requirement. [AWS performance-aligned success criteria](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf01-bp01.html) (Agentic AI Lens current), accessed 2026-09-30.

Agree the audit purpose and consequential decision/change classes needed to reconstruct actions under the approved policy, with permitted actor/version/result/correlation information, content minimization, access and retention rules. Assign runtime/change/review authority here. Aspect 4.4 refines concrete fields, redaction/export and correlation; 5.5 verifies deployed capture and 6.6 refines ongoing audit review. [OpenTelemetry sensitive-data guidance](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/security/handling-sensitive-data/) (updated 2026-01-14), [Microsoft responsible AI in Azure workloads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/responsible-ai) (current), and [AWS prompt and configuration lifecycle](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentops02.html) (Agentic AI Lens current), accessed 2026-09-30.

The obligation matrix is the authoritative applicability and required-control-outcome record, linked to later engineering controls and evidence rather than a second control design. The governance decision owns runtime/change authority and audit purpose/exposure/retention. NIST treats govern, map, measure and manage as context-dependent outcomes rather than a fixed checklist. [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) (AI RMF 1.0), accessed 2026-09-30. The cost/latency decision gives the per-task ceiling and selected latency signal (for example, time to first token, end-to-end task completion, or batch deadline), boundary, percentile or attainment goal when applicable, and window; P95 is one possible percentile rather than a universal choice. [AWS performance-aligned success criteria](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf01-bp01.html) (Agentic AI Lens current), accessed 2026-09-30.

## Architecture pattern catalogue (1.5)

Candidate patterns include single-agent, ReAct, Plan-and-Execute, Multi-Agent, Router + Skill, blackboard, and graph/workflow. These are composable candidates rather than seven required tiers. A direct model call or deterministic code path is also a candidate. A fixed workflow fits predictable steps; a model-directed loop fits uncertain next actions; routing fits separable task classes; parallel workers fit independent bounded subtasks; a stateful graph fits explicit branching, cycles, recovery, or approval. A graph need not be a DAG. Compare candidates on representative task success, cost, latency, error recovery, and operating burden. [Anthropic building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024-12-19), accessed 2026-09-30.

Candidate multi-agent classes include centralized/decentralized control, pipeline, supervisor, peer or shared-state approaches. Use task dependencies and writable resources to judge whether coordination is justified and name the integration owner in the coupling assessment. The selected topology, launch/join, delegation and partial-failure transitions are refined in 2.3; runtime concurrency and deployed capacity are later decisions. [AWS efficient workflow orchestration](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf05-bp01.html) (Agentic AI Lens current) and [Anthropic building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024-12-19), accessed 2026-09-30.

### Coupling and default choice

- Start with the simplest measured solution, including a direct call, deterministic workflow, or single agent with tools for a Skill library; add routing or multiple agents when representative tasks show selection errors, incompatible needs, context pressure, or a quality gain that pays for coordination. [Anthropic effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024-12-19) and [OpenAI practical agent guide](https://openai.com/business/guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-ai-agents/) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.
- Assess task coupling from dependent decisions and shared mutable effects. Record parallel feasibility only when independent state and an accountable integrator can preserve consistency; record ordered or coordinated work for dependent/conflicting effects. Aspect 2.3 refines executable launch/join and failure transitions rather than redoing this architectural feasibility judgment. [AWS efficient workflow orchestration](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/agentperf05-bp01.html) (Agentic AI Lens current) and [Anthropic orchestrator-workers](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024-12-19), accessed 2026-09-30.
- Choose only the architecture capabilities needed for long paths: fixed control, planning, local tool loops, durable state, recovery and approval. Compare which runtime supplies them. Aspect 2.3 defines executable stop/verification paths, 3.2 checkpoint contracts and 5.4 deployed persistence/resume guarantees. [Anthropic building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024-12-19) and [LangGraph interrupts](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/interrupts) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.

### Framework comparison (1.5)

Candidates include LangGraph, CrewAI, Microsoft Agent Framework, and PydanticAI. Also compare direct SDK/API calls or ordinary workflow code. Build the comparison from approved tasks: transition and loop control, durable checkpoint/resume, human approval, typing and validation, integration reach, deployment constraints, and operating burden. Check each candidate against its current documentation rather than treating historical rankings or ecosystem labels as timeless. [Anthropic building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024-12-19), accessed 2026-09-30.

LangGraph is an example of persisted graph state and interrupt/resume, but it requires a configured checkpointer, and production pause/resume needs a durable backend. Microsoft Agent Framework also offers checkpoints. A LangChain-ephemeral/LangGraph-persistent dichotomy should not be used as a product-wide rule; identify the actual runtime and state backend. The LangChain module inventory, component lens, and LLM-versus-agent framing remain background vocabulary, not selection criteria by themselves. [LangGraph persistence](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence) and [interrupts](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/interrupts) (current), and [Microsoft Agent Framework checkpoints](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/agent-framework/workflows/checkpoints) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.

### Harness architecture (1.5)

If a harness is needed, choose the component-level owner of state, execution, authority, recovery and observability and show required trust/approval boundaries in the architecture sketch. Aspect 2.6 refines permission enforcement, 2.9 execution isolation and threat controls, 4.4 telemetry, and Stage 5 deployed placement. Gateways, distributed execution, queues and memory services remain optional components whose operating burden must be justified. [OpenAI Agents API architecture](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/agents-api/architecture) (current) and [AWS Agentic AI Lens security design principles](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/agentic-ai-lens/security-design-principles.html) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.

Record the selected high-level architecture and runtime, simpler viable alternatives, support evidence, operating trade-offs and decision owner. Draw component control/data flow, state ownership and required approval boundaries without executable node/guard definitions. Each coupling row names task prerequisites, shared writable effects, concurrency feasibility and integration owner; 2.3 supplies the concrete sequencing and merge behavior. [Azure architecture decision records](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/architect-role/architecture-decision-record) (updated 2026-04-13), [architecture design specification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/architect-role/architecture-design-specification) (updated 2025-12-09), and [AI agent orchestration patterns](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/ai-ml/guide/ai-agent-design-patterns) (updated 2026-02-12), accessed 2026-09-30.

## Technique scope rules (1.6)

- **Retrieval.** Compare no retrieval with feasible evidence-access options on representative question classes to decide whether external evidence is needed. Record coverage, freshness and authority constraints in the technique scope row. Aspect 2.5 selects the direct-source or indexed route and ingestion/search topology, while Stage 3 refines segmentation and storage settings. [AWS document-query options](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/retrieval-augmented-generation-options/options.html) (Prescriptive Guidance current) and [Azure RAG preparation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/ai-ml/guide/rag/rag-preparation-phase) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Skill and live connection.** Decide independently whether a reusable procedure and live capability access are needed for an approved task. Record required capability and authority/integration constraints, then hand application-function, direct API, provider-tool or MCP selection to 2.2 and exact metadata/calls to 3.1. [OpenAI plugin architecture](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/concepts/plugins) (current) and [OpenAI Agents SDK tools](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/tools/) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Durable memory.** Decide whether an approved task requires intentional cross-session recall within 1.3/1.4 authority and data constraints. Record the purpose, inclusion and 2.4 owner; a session context alone does not require durable memory. Aspect 2.4 defines content, provenance, retention/correction/deletion policy, 2.6 access enforcement and 2.9 poisoning/trust controls. [Microsoft agentic-memory safety](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/security/zero-trust/sfi/manage-agentic-memory-safety) (updated 2026-06-03), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Modality.** Decide which tasks require image, audio, or video inputs or outputs. A CLIP/ViT/Q-Former encoder and adapter route is a candidate for custom model development, not a prerequisite to multimodal use. First check supported model/API capabilities and task results; defer preprocessing and any demonstrated custom training or adapter need to later design. [OpenAI image and vision](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/images-vision) and [audio](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/audio) (OpenAI API v1 current), accessed 2026-09-30.
- **Decision handoff.** A Skill may bundle instructions with optional supporting resources; it does not imply a separate router. Router + Skill remains a candidate only when discovery or selection errors justify it. Record each yes/no scope choice, the approved task and evidence, the downstream owner, and which optional work is omitted when declined. General tool, context, and integration aspects still apply even if MCP or durable memory is declined. [Anthropic effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024-12-19) and [OpenAI practical agent guide](https://openai.com/business/guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-ai-agents/) (current), accessed 2026-09-30.
